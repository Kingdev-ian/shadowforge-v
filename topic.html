Demand & Supply

Master how markets find their balance.

The Avocado Crisis

Imagine you wake up craving avocado toast. You head to the market, wallet in hand. But overnight, a frost wiped out half the avocado farms in the country.

The vendor looks at you with tired eyes. Prices went up. You stare at the sign — $8 per avocado. Yesterday it was $2.

You have just witnessed the Law of Supply. When supply drops and demand stays the same — prices rise. The market has spoken.

The Shift

Now imagine everyone in your city goes on a health kick. Suddenly everyone wants avocados. Demand shoots up. Supply hasn't changed. Prices rise again.

Farmers see the high prices and plant more avocados. Six months later supply catches up. Prices fall. That dance between buyers and sellers finding balance — that is Equilibrium.

Core Definitions

Demand

The quantity of a good consumers are willing and able to buy at various prices, all else being equal.

Supply

The quantity of a good producers are willing and able to offer for sale at various prices.

Equilibrium Price

The price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. The market clears — no shortage or surplus exists.

The Laws

Law of Demand
as price rises, quantity demanded falls.
Law of Supply
as price rises, quantity supplied rises.
At equilibrium
quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.
Shortage
price below equilibrium, demand exceeds supply.
Surplus
price above equilibrium, supply exceeds demand.
